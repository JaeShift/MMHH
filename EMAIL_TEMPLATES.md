**Your Micro-Habit for Today:**

**Why This Matters:**
Proper hydration supports your body's natural rhythms and hormone production. Setting a daily intention helps align your actions with your values, reducing stress and increasing clarity.

**Quick Tip:**
Keep a water bottle with you throughout the day. Aim for half your body weight in ounces of water (e.g., if you weigh 150 lbs, aim for 75 oz).

## Day 2: The Sleep-Hormone Connection

**Subject:** Day 2: The Sleep-Hormone Connection - Your Micro-Habit Challenge

**Body:**
Hi [Name],

It's Day 2! How did Day 1 go?

Today, we're focusing on **The Sleep-Hormone Connection**.

**Your Micro-Habit for Today:**
Create a consistent bedtime routine. Set a "wind down" time 30 minutes before bed—no screens, dim the lights, and do something calming (read, stretch, journal).

**Why This Matters:**
Sleep directly impacts your hormone production, especially cortisol, melatonin, and growth hormone. Poor sleep can worsen anxiety, depression, and hormonal imbalances. A consistent routine signals to your body that it's time to rest.

**Quick Tip:**
Keep your bedroom cool (around 65-68°F) and dark. Consider using blackout curtains or a sleep mask.

**Your Challenge:**
Stick to the same bedtime and wake time (even on weekends) for the next 7 days. Notice how this affects your energy and mood.

## Day 3: Blood Sugar & Mood Stability

**Subject:** Day 3: Blood Sugar & Mood Stability - Your Micro-Habit Challenge

**Body:**
Hi [Name],

You're on Day 3! How are you feeling?

Today, we're focusing on **Blood Sugar & Mood Stability**.

**Your Micro-Habit for Today:**
Eat a balanced breakfast within an hour of waking. Include protein, healthy fats, and complex carbs (e.g., eggs with whole grain toast and avocado, or Greek yogurt with berries and nuts).

**Why This Matters:**
Blood sugar spikes and crashes can trigger anxiety, irritability, and mood swings—especially during hormonal transitions. Starting your day with balanced nutrition stabilizes your blood sugar and supports steady energy and mood throughout the day.

**Quick Tip:**
If you're not hungry in the morning, start with something small. Your body will adjust. Avoid skipping breakfast—it sets the tone for your entire day.

**Your Challenge:**
Notice how your energy and mood feel more stable when you fuel your body consistently. Track any changes in your workbook.

## Day 4: Taming the Stress Hormone

**Subject:** Day 4: Taming the Stress Hormone - Your Micro-Habit Challenge

**Body:**
Hi [Name],

Day 4 is here! You're more than halfway through.

Today, we're focusing on **Taming the Stress Hormone**.

**Your Micro-Habit for Today:**
Practice 5 minutes of deep breathing or meditation. Try the 4-7-8 technique: inhale for 4 counts, hold for 7, exhale for 8. Repeat 4 times.

**Why This Matters:**
Chronic stress keeps your cortisol levels elevated, which can disrupt sleep, worsen anxiety, and throw your hormones out of balance. Deep breathing activates your parasympathetic nervous system (the "rest and digest" response), signaling safety to your body.

**Quick Tip:**
Set a reminder on your phone for the same time each day. Even 5 minutes can make a significant difference in how you handle stress.

**Your Challenge:**
Use this breathing technique whenever you feel overwhelmed or anxious. Notice how it helps you respond rather than react.

Day 5! You're building real momentum.

Today, we're focusing on **The Gut-Brain Axis**.

**Your Micro-Habit for Today:**
Add one probiotic-rich or fiber-rich food to one meal today. Examples: yogurt, kefir, sauerkraut, kimchi, or a serving of vegetables like broccoli, asparagus, or beans.

**Why This Matters:**
Your gut and brain communicate constantly through the gut-brain axis. A healthy gut microbiome supports mood regulation, reduces inflammation, and helps balance hormones. Many women with anxiety, depression, or hormonal issues also have gut imbalances.

**Quick Tip:**
You don't need expensive supplements. Start with whole foods. If you're new to fermented foods, start small to let your gut adjust.

**Your Challenge:**
Notice how your digestion and mood feel when you prioritize gut health. Track any changes in your workbook.

**Subject:** Day 6: Movement for Mental Health - Your Micro-Habit Challenge

**Body:**
Hi [Name],

Day 6! You're in the home stretch.

Today, we're focusing on **Movement for Mental Health**.

**Your Micro-Habit for Today:**
Move your body for at least 10 minutes. This could be a walk, gentle stretching, dancing to your favorite song, or any movement that feels good to you.

**Why This Matters:**
Movement boosts endorphins, reduces cortisol, improves sleep, and supports hormone balance. You don't need intense workouts—even gentle movement signals to your body that you're safe and capable.

**Quick Tip:**
If you're feeling low energy, start with just 5 minutes. The goal is consistency, not intensity. Movement should feel supportive, not punishing.

**Your Challenge:**
Find a type of movement you actually enjoy. When movement feels good, you're more likely to stick with it.

## Notes for Implementation:

1. **PDF Attachment:** You'll need to add the PDF path to the welcome email in the API route
2. **Zapier Setup:** 
   - Use the `/api/7daychallenge` endpoint to capture signups
   - Set up a Zapier workflow that:
     - Triggers on new signup
     - Adds to your lead book/CRM
     - Sends the 7-day email sequence (one per day)
3. **Email Service:** Consider using a service like Resend, SendGrid, or Mailgun for better deliverability and scheduling
4. **Database:** You may want to store signups in a database (like Supabase, PlanetScale, or MongoDB) to track who has received which emails
