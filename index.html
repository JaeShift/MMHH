Care designed for women in their 40s and 50s

Modern Mental Health & Hormones (MMHH) is a calm, clinical space led by nurse practitioner Stephanie Nichols, CNP. We blend mental health care and hormone evaluation so your plan makes sense for your life. Whether you're dealing with mood changes, brain fog, stress, or sleep disruption, you'll work one‑on‑one with a single provider who listens, explains, and follows up—so you always know the next step.

What makes MMHH different

  • Whole‑person approach: mind, body, hormones, lifestyle
  • Clear plans with practical steps
  • "Start low, go slow" when using medications
  • Lab‑informed decisions when indicated

Services

Mid‑Life Hormone Consultation (60 min)

History, symptom mapping, review of cycle changes, medication/supplement check, and labs if indicated.

Includes: Options from behavioral tools to menopausal hormone therapy (MHT) when appropriate, with a written plan.

Follow‑up: 4–8 weeks, then every 3–6 months.

Mood & Anxiety Evaluation (45–60 min)

Targeted screening, review of contributors (sleep, hormones, life stress), and a collaborative plan.

Includes: CBT‑style skills; medication if appropriate; clear safety plan.

Follow‑up: 4–6 weeks initially.

Medication Management (25 min)

Fine‑tuning response, minimizing side effects, and tracking results with shared decisions.

Follow‑up: Every 1–3 months depending on stability.

Sleep & Stress Reset (45 min)

Restore sleep quality and regulate stress systems: schedule, light, movement, nutrition, and behavioral tools.

Follow‑up: 4–6 weeks, then as needed.

Lifestyle & Prevention Coaching (30 min)

Heart‑brain‑metabolism in mid‑life; practical, sustainable changes, goals, and accountability.

Follow‑up: Monthly or quarterly.

Hormones & Mood in Mid‑Life

Perimenopause and menopause can influence serotonin, sleep, and stress systems—changing how you feel day to day. You're not imagining it, and you're not alone.

  • Assessment: history & symptom mapping, screening tools, medication review, and labs when indicated.
  • Care Options: cognitive & behavioral strategies, SSRIs/SNRIs or other medications when appropriate, MHT for eligible patients, and sleep/nutrition/movement skills.
  • Shared Decisions: we discuss risks, benefits, and preferences—your plan fits your goals.

Common Questions

Is it my hormones or something else?

Often it's a mix. We sort through contributors and test when useful.

What labs might help?

Depends on history and symptoms (e.g., CBC, CMP, TSH, ferritin, B12, vitamin D).

Do you offer virtual visits?

We offer in-person appointments in Columbus.
